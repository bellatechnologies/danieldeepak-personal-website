From engineer
to business enabler.

I spent 15 years in development and DevOps before shifting focus to helping businesses take advantage of the tools available to them.

Most of that time went into PHP, Laravel, Python, React and PostgreSQL. I authored WordPress plugins, built subscription-based course platforms, worked on infrastructure migrations and picked up .NET along the way. Polyglot is an accurate description.

“Shipping work that actually moves a business is a different feeling from shipping code that works.”

The work that sticks with me most came from my time at Anatta. I was part of the team that helped Rothy’s scale from zero to $220M in annual revenue. I also helped a client move off Rackspace onto self-hosted infrastructure, saving them $18,000 a year.

At some point I started noticing that most businesses aren’t held back by what technology can do. They don’t know what exists, or they haven’t connected the right pieces, or they simply don’t have the bandwidth to set it up. AI has changed what’s possible here and I wanted to be on the side that helps businesses take that leverage.

Current focus

Right now I focus on spa and wellness businesses. It’s an industry that is growing fast and is ready for better tooling. Most business owners are still handling enquiries and client follow-ups manually and there’s a lot of ground to cover.

Outside of client work, I keep exploring servers and cloud infrastructure, reading about business models, and occasionally shipping side projects.
